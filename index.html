About Angela Leavitt

Angela Leavitt is a designer and entrepreneur recognized for pioneering the cigar box handbag trend. What began as handbags created from repurposed cigar boxes evolved into a distinctive accessories brand known for its vintage-inspired aesthetic, creative use of fabrics, and collectible designs.

Angela Leavitt Handbags were carried by more than 100 retailers throughout the United States and internationally, including Fred Segal, Henri Bendel, Macy's, Betsey Johnson, BCBG, and Four Seasons gift shops. The brand also attracted a notable clientele that included Elton John, Mila Kunis, Gwen Stefani, Christina Aguilera, Sarah Michelle Gellar, Denise Richards, Susan Sarandon, and Brittany Murphy.
